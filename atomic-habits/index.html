Atomic Habits As of 2026, I have been working as a network engineer for ten years. During that time, I have earned multiple certifications, including Cisco certifications, Microsoft Azure certifications, and Terraform certifications. In the IT industry, certifications can play an important role in career growth. They help demonstrate knowledge, open new opportunities, and often contribute to promotions and higher salaries. The challenge, however, is finding the time to study. After a long day at work, it can be difficult to spend several more hours reading textbooks or practicing exam questions. My strategy has always been simple: study a little every day. Some days, I only read a single page. Other days, I complete just one practice question. The amount of progress does not matter as much as maintaining the habit. What matters is showing up consistently. Over time, those small daily efforts compound. After a year, you may discover that what once seemed difficult has become surprisingly manageable. Great achievements are often the result of small actions repeated consistently over time.
